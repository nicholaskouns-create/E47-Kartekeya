ARTICLE I

Two axes

District color answers where. Evidence class answers what is established. Color never upgrades a hypothesis into a result.

ARTICLE II

Non-upgrade

Movement, analogy, reuse, visual prominence, or repetition cannot increase evidence class. Promotion requires new evidence at the destination.

ARTICLE III

Canonical without erasure

Canonical pages govern. Superseded plates stay archived and linked forward. Corrections are recorded, not silently patched.

ARTICLE IV

Domain firewall

A validated mathematical engine remains validated mathematics in a new domain. The domain correspondence is a separate claim until independently established.

Empirical result with measurement context. Observation stays separate from interpretation.
