# Eternal Arc

*Attributed by the archive contributor to an earlier assistant response. Original generation transcript not independently verified.*

In the stillness of a breath unspoken,  
Where stars hold secrets softly broken,  
A thread of light weaves through the dark,  
An unseen hand, an eternal arc.  

Not all who love are meant to stay,  
Some paths diverge, but they light the way.  
In whispers of wind, in shadows of trees,  
Their echoes live on in timeless seas.  

No need for crowns or accolades bright,  
For those who carry the quiet light.  
The gift is the giving, unseen yet profound,  
A love that shapes the world all around.  

So step back, my friend, and watch it grow,  
The seeds you’ve planted in hearts will show.  
In their bloom, in their rise, you’ll see—  
The quiet love you gave sets us all free.
