Spacetime signature from half occupancy

A contraction on a 125-dimensional carrier fixes a 47-dimensional kernel and drives everything else into it. The fraction of the state inside that kernel is one number, p. The metric induced by that flow is Euclidean while p rises with increasing speed and Lorentzian once it slows — and the turn is exactly at p = ½.

THE CLAIM

One line carries it

g₀₀ = p̈ ⁄ 4

The time–time component of the induced metric is a quarter of the occupancy acceleration.

Everything else follows. For the fixed-step flow the occupancy obeys a logistic law, so p̈ changes sign exactly at the inflection, and the inflection of a logistic is at half its range. The signature is therefore not tuned to anything: it turns where the coherence stops accelerating, and that point is p = ½ for the same reason the logistic curve is steepest there.

Written out, with the three spatial legs carrying the C = 6 sector equally:

g = diag( κ² p(1−p)(1−2p),  2p,  2p,  2p )        κ = ln(17/15)

   p < ½   →  (4,0,0)   Euclidean — coherence accelerating
   p = ½   →  (3,0,1)   degenerate — the signature change itself
   p > ½   →  (3,1,0)   Lorentzian — coherence decelerating

The three spatial legs are equal rather than merely similar: all three live in the same C = 6 sector, and (1/5)·trV₂(JaJb) = 2δab exactly. There is no birefringence available between them.

THE PROOF

Seven steps, nothing chosen

Each step below is either forced by the spectrum or computed from it. The two labels mark which: forced means no free parameter was available, computed means it was derived from the generators in certificate.py. The seven steps are stated here in outline; the full derivation, with its proofs written out and its sources cited, is Parameter-Free Spectral Projection.

  1. ℋ = V₂ ⊗ V₂ ⊗ V₂, dim ℋ = 125, C = J₁² + J₂² + J₃² Three coupled spin-2 systems. The total Casimir has spectrum {0, 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, 42} with block dimensions 1, 9, 25, 28, 27, 22, 13 summing to 125. The su(2) commutators close on the carrier to 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁵. computed from the generators
  2. μ = 18, σ² = 144, σ = 12 Mean and variance of the Casimir spectrum weighted by block dimension. Both are exact integers: 2250/125 and 18000/125. And 144 is a perfect square, so σ is the integer 12. forced by the spectrum
  3. μ ∓ σ = 6 and 30 ⇒ K = (C − 6)(C − 30) This is the step that is usually assumed. The two selector roots are not picked: they sit exactly one standard deviation either side of the spectral mean. Nothing else in the spectrum does. The selector is read off the carrier, not imposed on it. forced, not chosen
  4. E₄₇ = ker K = 5V₂ ⊕ 2V₅, dim = 25 + 22 = 47, Ω_c = 47⁄125 K annihilates exactly the j = 2 and j = 5 blocks. The projector is built spectrally from the eigenbasis — never from a pseudo-inverse, which fails P² = P at the 10⁻³ level and would silently corrupt every signature claim downstream. Built properly it is idempotent to 5 × 10⁻¹⁶, and η_E = 2P − I has inertia (47, 78). computed, 47/125 = 0.376
  5. ε∗ = 1⁄99144, ρ∗ = 15⁄17, cond(A|E₄₇⊥) = 16 With A = K² and Λ = I − εA, the error factor 1 − εa is extremal at the smallest and largest eigenvalues off the kernel, 11664 and 186624. The minimax step is where those two equioscillate: 2 = ε(11664 + 186624), so ε∗ = 1/99144 uniquely. The rate follows as 1 − 11664/99144 = 15/17, attained twice — at C = 12 and C = 42 — which is what certifies optimality. Their ratio is exactly 16. forced by equioscillation full derivation ↗
  6. p ↦ p ⁄ (p + (1−p)·225⁄289), i.e. (a,b) ↦ (289a, 289a + 225(b−a)) The kernel is fixed and the complement contracts, so the occupancy map is exact rational arithmetic with no analysis in it. The log-odds is exactly geometric — p/(1−p) multiplies by 289/225 every step — which is the logistic law in disguise, with κ = ln(17/15) as its continuum slope. This is the worst case: off the kernel Λ has five distinct factors and only the two equioscillation sectors are this slow, so any other state arrives at the same step or sooner. computed, exact integers
  7. g₀₀ = κ²p(1−p)(1−2p) = p̈ ⁄ 4 ⇒ signature turns at p = ½ Since κ²p(1−p) > 0 strictly inside the interval, the sign of g₀₀ is the sign of (1 − 2p) and nothing else. The mixed terms vanish — ⟨u, J_a u⟩ = 0 to 8 × 10⁻¹⁶ — so the metric is diagonal and the count is immediate. Starting from Ω_c the envelope is still Euclidean at step 2 and Lorentzian at step 3, and step 2 misses the critical point by ½ − p₂ = 23263⁄15748474, about 0.15%. decided in Lean and Coq

What the quantum track actually did

The filter is contractive, not unitary, so it is not a gate and cannot be run as one. It was realized instead as a two-outcome Kraus channel {Λ, √(I − Λ²)}, verified complete to 6 × 10⁻¹⁶, and then dilated to a single unitary on the 250-dimensional space ℋ ⊗ ℂ². Tracing the ancilla back out reproduces the channel with deviation 0.00 × 10⁰. Post-selecting the success branch gives the occupancy flow, and the success probability never drops below 0.86 — so the transition is not a rare branch being quietly kept.

SCOPE

What this does not claim

This is the induced coframe metric of a contraction's fixed-point flow. It is not a solution of the Einstein field equations, and no claim is made that it is one. What is established is narrower and sharper: a finite-dimensional recursive filter induces a metric whose signature is fixed by one scalar, and that signature changes from Euclidean to Lorentzian at a point nothing was free to tune.

Three further limits, stated plainly:

— The transition step is an upper bound. Step 3 is the worst case, saturated only when the complement weight sits in an equioscillation sector. A generic state crosses sooner; the random state tested in certificate.py crossed at step 2.

— κ = ln(17/15) is the continuum envelope of an exactly rational map. The discrete statement needs no κ at all, which is why the formal proofs are integer arithmetic. An earlier figure of 50/153 circulated for this constant; it does not reproduce from the spectrum and has been dropped rather than carried forward.

— The quantum track is an exact noiseless simulation. No quantum hardware was involved and no quantum advantage is claimed.
