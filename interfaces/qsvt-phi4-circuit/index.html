The full demonstrated circuit

Switch between the five-phase QSP chain, the 500-dimensional LCU superstructure, and the mathematical block semantics.

Z(φ) acts on the QSP signal ancilla. W(A) acts jointly on that ancilla and the 125D carrier. The upper-left 125D block is complex P(A), not directly Q₄(A).

One LCU ancilla is added outside the existing QSP signal ancilla. Total exact Hilbert-space dimension: 2 × 2 × 125 = 500. A physically allocated 9-qubit register has 512 states, of which 12 require defined padding behavior.
