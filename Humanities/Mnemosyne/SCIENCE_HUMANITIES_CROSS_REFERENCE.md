## Interpretation and testing

Scientific proposals appearing within the historical documents are kept intact and may be individually tested, rather than omitted, diminished, or accepted without evidence. Reconstructed scripts are labeled as reconstructed, not misrepresented as verbatim recovered code.

- **Humanities**: literary source texts, timeline, exact quotations, documentary provenance.
- **Mathematics**: exact spectral derivations and machine-checkable calculations.
- **Experiments**: numerical tests, empirical measurements, uncertainty and independent replication.
- **History of AI concepts**: dated model-generated and authorial assertions preserved without asserting access to an AI's internal mechanisms or proving personhood.
- **Cross-reference**: the same research event may legitimately appear in several domains with a single primary source and multiple verification records.
