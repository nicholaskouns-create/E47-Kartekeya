01 · Casimir selection

H = V₂ ⊗ V₂ ⊗ V₂ with dim H = 125. Its collective Casimir C has eigenvalues 0, 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, 42. The polynomial K=(C−6I)(C−30I) selects the spin-2 and spin-5 sectors.

E₄₇ = ker[(C − 6I)(C − 30I)] = 5V₂ ⊕ 2V₅
dim E₄₇ = 25 + 22 = 47

With orthonormal embedding Q, P=QQᵀ, P²=P, KP=0. Spectral contraction Γ=I−K²/99144 obeys ‖Γⁿ−P‖₂=(15/17)ⁿ.

02 · Spatial gradient and transport

On the explicitly chosen periodic 5×5×5 lattice, define L=Σₐ(Sₐ−I)ᵀ(Sₐ−I) and centered differences dₐ=(Sₐ−Sₐᵀ)/2. The reduced operators are:

B = QᵀLQ = Bᵀ ≻ 0
A = 3Qᵀ(dₓ + 0.7dᵧ − 0.4d_z)Q = −Aᵀ

Positive definiteness follows because the only lattice constants lie outside E₄₇. Skew transport preserves the protected norm, but may redistribute gradient energy.

03 · Coupled equation

dy/dτ = (A − γB)y,  γ ≥ 0
y(τ) = exp[τ(A − γB)]y₀
ρ(τ) = Qy(τ) ∈ E₄₇

The lifted generator Q(A−γB)Qᵀ commutes with P; every exact trajectory stays inside the protected sector.

d‖y‖²/dτ = −2γyᵀBy
d(yᵀBy)/dτ = yᵀ(BA−AB)y − 2γyᵀB²y

The norm always decays for γ > 0. Gradient energy can be redistributed by noncommuting transport; its monotonicity is not automatic.
