## Theorem 2 — exact E47-indexed vacuum family

Let \(B=(b_0,\ldots,b_{46})\) be the deterministic real orthonormal basis of
\(E_{47}\) obtained by pivot Gram-Schmidt of the columns \(P_{47}e_n\) in
lexicographic cube order. For
\[

\[

\[
F_q(u)=u^{78}+u^{47}+\sum_{a=0}^{46}c_a(q)u^a.

\[
R_{uu}=-\frac12(H_{xx}+H_{yy})=0,
\]
and every other Ricci component vanishes. Thus \(g_q\) is an exact
non-flat Ricci-flat Lorentzian metric.

## Theorem 3 — injection into the completely unmarked Einstein moduli space

Let
\[
\Theta:E_{47}^{\mathbb R}\to
\operatorname{Ein}_{\rm vac}(M_E)/\operatorname{Diff}(M_E),
\qquad
q\mapsto[g_q].
\]

The reduction to the residual profile freedom is intrinsic, so no Brinkmann marking is retained in the theorem. For every non-flat member of this family, the parallel null line is unique. Indeed, at any point with \(F_q(u)\neq0\), the screen curvature endomorphism is \(F_q(u)D\) with \(D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\), hence it is invertible on the two-dimensional screen. A parallel vector annihilated by all curvature endomorphisms therefore has no screen component, leaving only the parallel null line. Since an isometry maps parallel vector fields to parallel vector fields, it preserves this null line.

Let \(k\) span that intrinsic line. Its dual parallel one-form is proportional to \(du\). Thus any isometry satisfies \(u\mapsto au+b\) with constant \(a\neq0\). The quotient screen bundle \(k^\perp/\langle k\rangle\) carries the induced Euclidean metric and parallel connection, so an isometry acts on a parallel screen frame by a constant \(O\in O(2)\). Wave-front Heisenberg translations change only the choice of origin in a wavefront and do not change the curvature profile. Consequently, if \([g_q]=[g_r]\), the curvature endomorphisms obey
\[
F_q(u)D=a^2F_r(au+b)\,O^TDO,\qquad D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1).
\]
Because the left side is a scalar multiple of \(D\) wherever \(F_q\neq0\), the orthogonal conjugate must satisfy \(O^TDO=sD\) with \(s=\pm1\). Hence
\[
F_q(u)=s\,a^2F_r(au+b).
\]

Compare polynomial coefficients.

Degree 78:
\[
1=s\,a^{80}.
\]
Thus \(s=1\) and \(|a|=1\).

Degree 77:
\[
0=78a^{79}b,
\]
so \(b=0\).

Degree 47:
\[
1=a^{49},
\]
so \(a=1\).

All lower coefficients therefore satisfy
\[
c_a(q)=c_a(r),\quad a=0,\ldots,46,
\]
hence \(q=r\).

Therefore \(\Theta\) is injective. Its inverse on the image is obtained by
taking the unique profile representative normalized by the \(u^{78}\) and
\(u^{47}\) coefficients and reading the remaining 47 coefficients.

Thus
\[
\boxed{
E_{47}^{\mathbb R}\cong\mathscr M_{E47}
\subset
\operatorname{Ein}_{\rm vac}(M_E)/\operatorname{Diff}(M_E)
}
\]
where \(\mathscr M_{E47}\) is a 47-real-dimensional unmarked vacuum-Einstein
subfamily.
