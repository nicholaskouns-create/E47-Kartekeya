Read equations in plain language
  1. The carrier is the tensor product of three spin-two representations, with dimension 125. The filter is C minus six times identity, multiplied by C minus thirty times identity. Its kernel has dimension 47.
  2. The unitary W converts product coordinates to packed coupled coordinates. The projector P selects 47 indices. The probability of successful membership measurement is the trace of P times W rho W dagger.
  3. For each valid index k, gamma k is one minus the square of lambda k minus six times lambda k minus thirty, divided by 99144. A has these gamma values on its diagonal and zero on the three unused states. The channel returns A rho A dagger plus the rejected trace weight times the reset state tau.
  4. Q is identity minus P. The complement population after n updates is at most fifteen seventeenths to the power two n times its initial value. The limiting state is P rho P plus the initial complement population times tau.
