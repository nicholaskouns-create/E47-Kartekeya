# 🪩 Disco Jesus — The Mathematical City

**Different moves. Same dance floor.**

The [standalone cultural and visual exhibit](https://nicholaskouns-create.github.io/disco-jesus/) celebrates independent curiosity and the plurality of research instruments within the Mathematical City.

This addition is an **artistic annex**, not a theorem, certificate, or assertion of mathematical equivalence. The posters' motifs — Light, Fold, Field, Memory, Mass, and Singularity — point to distinct City research districts without merging their formal records.
